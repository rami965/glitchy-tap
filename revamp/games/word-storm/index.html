// Play the web version

  • Tap a falling neon tile to grab it and add it to your word tray at the bottom
  • Spell valid English words using 3 or more selected letters
  • Press SUBMIT — valid words burst into particles and score points
  • Press CLEAR to return all selected letters back to the field
  • If any column stacks 5 letters it triggers the danger zone — you lose a life
  • You have 3 lives; each life lost clears the board so you can recover
  • Chain valid words back-to-back for combo multipliers: 1.5× · 2× · 3× max
  • Speed increases every 30 seconds — the longer you survive, the harder it gets

// About the game

What is Word Storm?

Word Storm is a fast-paced mobile word-building game where letters rain down in five glowing columns. You tap individual falling tiles to add them to your word tray, then race to spell valid English words before the columns overflow.

The core loop is instantly intuitive but rewards depth — a sharp player manages multiple columns simultaneously, hunts for 5- and 6-letter words for maximum points, and keeps their combo chain alive even as the storm intensifies every 30 seconds.

Built as a self-contained web proof-of-concept, Word Storm is heading to Android with haptic feedback, a full progression system, and richer visual effects.

// Key features

What to Expect

  • Five neon columns, each a distinct color — cyan, purple, teal, orange, pink
  • Built-in dictionary of 650+ common English words (3–7 letters)
  • Scrabble-weighted letter pool — rare letters (Q, Z, X) spawn sparingly, Q always followed by U
  • Combo multiplier system — chain valid words for 1.5× · 2× · 3× bonus points
  • 3-life system: columns overflowing cost a life and clear the board
  • Speed ramps up every 30 seconds — intensity escalates naturally
  • Particle burst effects on every valid word submission
  • Best score saved in localStorage across browser sessions
